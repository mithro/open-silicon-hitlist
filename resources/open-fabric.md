## Projects and repositories
- [FABulous](https://github.com/FPGA-Research/FABulous) — eFPGA framework (Heidelberg/Manchester)
- [OpenFPGA](https://github.com/lnis-uofu/OpenFPGA) — VPR-based fabric generator (Utah)
- [SOFA](https://github.com/lnis-uofu/SOFA) — OpenFPGA SKY130 tapeouts
- [PRGA](https://github.com/PrincetonUniversity/prga) — Princeton reconfigurable gate array
- [VTR](https://github.com/verilog-to-routing/vtr-verilog-to-routing) — architecture exploration engine
- [nextpnr](https://github.com/YosysHQ/nextpnr) — FABulous uses its generic/himbaechel path
- [OpenROAD](https://github.com/The-OpenROAD-Project/OpenROAD) and [LibreLane](https://github.com/librelane/librelane) — open hardening
- [wafer.space](https://wafer.space) and [Tiny Tapeout](https://tinytapeout.com) — cheap shuttles for fabric test chips
