# Common rules for every run

The same for both configurations. You are working on the open-silicon hit list, a public list of open hardware projects worth doing. Tim Ansell (GitHub user mithro) had the idea for the list; his documents, talks and experiments are the record most items rest on. Each item has a document, written from a source pack: a private file that lists every claim with its source, built before the document is written.

From the guidelines in force:

1. **Pack only.** Write from the source pack only; a claim not in the pack goes into the pack, with its source, before it is used.
2. **Source or status.** Every claim links to its source, or carries one sanctioned status. Never invent: write `None found (searched: …)`, `Unknown (settled by: …)` or `Not applicable (…)`.
3. **Nothing from memory.** Never cite a paper, repository or document from memory; resolve it with a tool and copy the identifier.
4. **Tim's claims checked.** Attribute to Tim only what you read in his documents, and check his claims as you would anyone else's.
